# PCR 6 — the right rail refuses to collapse

Keep the per-instance lane after the shared frame/corner support emerges.

A right-side rail/strip appears on some badge residuals, but unlike the corners it does not collapse into a clean shared explanation:

- its strength varies materially by course and instance;
- the strongest rail examples are not the same badge numbers across courses;
- it is not explained cleanly by the per-digit residual templates;
- it is not stable enough to be promoted into the shared frame support.

Therefore this checkpoint keeps the rail visible and explicitly unexplained.

No ontology is assigned. It may involve local background interaction, raster phase, layout/neighbor effects, another rendering layer, or something else. The PCR does not need that answer yet; it needs to preserve the anomaly instead of hiding it inside a broader mask.

This is the point of the progressive render: stable evidence gains custody, unstable evidence remains visible, and the unexplained remainder becomes the next empirical question rather than an architectural guess.
