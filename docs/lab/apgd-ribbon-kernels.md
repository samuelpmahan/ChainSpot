# APGD ribbon-kernel discovery checkpoint

Base: `cb160a5d5f3d68df272770044b30ff4f34e7225a` (`lab: make dark badge plates primary`).

## Scope / warning

This checkpoint is **not** an exact D08 replay and is **not** a new Dev72 assignment claim. The D08 binary cache was not mounted in the execution runtime. The discovery harness therefore uses the four exact annotated rasters and constructs synthetic hard rivals that deliberately ride neighboring **real annotated ribbons** after a connector. Ground truth is used to construct/evaluate these rivals. The experiment answers: *which pixel/ribbon evidence families look underused and transfer leave-one-course-out?* It does not answer final ownership accuracy.

No images were retained or generated by the experiment. Every accepted experimental run was guarded at 40 seconds. A monolithic triplet run reached the 40 s guard once, was killed, marked invalid, and deleted; all later triplets were split to one configuration per run.

## Kernel bank

The discovery bank samples a normalized transverse strip at offsets `[-1.25,-1,-.8,-.65,-.5,-.35,-.2,0,.2,.35,.5,.65,.8,1,1.25] * W` every 7 px along a candidate route. Known badge/tee/basket object neighborhoods are excluded before scoring.

Evidence families tried:

- fitted alpha-composite profile toward a neutral corridor paint color
- alpha lower-tail profile (`q25`)
- local luminance lift profile
- center-vs-outer contrast
- paired left/right rail transitions and single-edge imbalance
- profile symmetry, center-of-mass, fitted width second moment
- generative alpha-reconstruction residual
- contrast/rail persistence: low fraction and longest low run
- Tee->Badge versus Badge->Basket early/late summaries
- symmetry-tied transverse kernels
- eight longitudinal bins per structural sequence
- longitudinal cosine modes 1..3
- normalized total variation and lag-1 correlation
- exact badge-split Tee->Badge and Badge->Basket leg kernels

Every scalar is eligible for non-monotonic response. Basis families tried: linear, `[z,z^2]`, `[z,z^2,|z|]`, three-hinge + quadratic, 5-center RBF + quadratic, and quantile quadratic splines. This is intentional: many renderer measurements are plausibly U-shaped (too little and too much are both bad evidence).

## Main findings

### 1. U-shaped capacity is real, but shallow capacity wins

Initial static ribbon-only LOCO rank-1:

- linear: 48/72
- quadratic U: 50/72
- hinge: 51/72
- RBF: 52/72
- one-step hard-negative refit: **42/72** (rejected; obvious overfit)

With the larger longitudinal bank:

- linear: 48/72
- U+abs: 49/72
- hinge: 51/72
- RBF: 52/72
- 4-knot quadratic spline: **57/72**
- 5-knot quadratic spline: 52/72

The 4->5 knot regression is the important guardrail: useful nonlinearity is not evidence for more optimization.

### 2. A few physical ribbon families carry most of the signal

Static group-only / ablation work repeatedly points at:

- inner/outer alpha
- paired rails
- center-vs-outer contrast / raw alpha profile

A compact 3-family model `inner_outer_alpha + paired_rails + alpha_profile` gives:

- linear: 50/72
- quadratic U: 55/72
- U + abs: 57/72
- RBF: **58/72**
- 4-knot spline: 57/72
- 5-knot spline: 54/72

Removing paired rails from the comparable compact trio drops to 50/72. Paired rails are therefore a strong interaction bridge rather than just another correlated summary.

Static-only versus longitudinal-only for these three families, using 4-knot spline:

- static: 55/72
- longitudinal: 56/72
- combined: 57/72

So longitudinal position is useful, but the full 247-feature kitchen sink is unnecessary.

### 3. One crude persistence scalar is shockingly strong

`contrast_frac_low` by itself reaches **52/72** LOCO rank-1. `paired_edge_q25` reaches 51/72. This strongly suggests the current scorer underuses *how much of the candidate route remains consistently ribbon-like* and the lower tail of two-rail support.

Several longitudinal measurements are weak linearly but become materially better with U/RBF transforms (for example `inner_resid_lag1`, `inner_alpha_cos3`, and `contrast_cos3`). These are not strong enough alone to promote, but they falsify “no signal”: their relationship to truth is non-monotonic.

### 4. Learned U-shaped likelihoods work

A diagonal Gaussian truth-vs-rival density ratio (one quadratic log-likelihood curve per raw feature) reaches 54/72. Feeding those learned U-shaped likelihood coordinates into Ridge reaches **55/72**, mean rank 1.94. Histogram likelihood transforms were weaker.

This is a particularly clean interpretation of the user's U-shaped warning: learn `log P(feature|truth) - log P(feature|rival)` per feature, then linearly combine those transformed testimonies.

### 5. Absolute course scale is partly nuisance

On the compact three-family bank, truth-free relative normalization improves transfer:

- raw linear: 50/72
- per-badge z linear: 55/72
- per-badge rank linear: 56/72
- per-course rank linear: **58/72**
- per-course rank RBF: 58/72 with mean rank 1.54

Robust MAD normalization mostly hurt. Relative rank evidence appears more stable than raw alpha/support magnitudes across basemap/course distributions.

### 6. Exact leg-conditioned ribbon evidence is individually strong

When the candidate route is split at the **actual badge vertex**, a 22-feature 4-knot spline gives:

- Tee owner from Tee->Badge ribbon only: **60/72**
- Basket owner from Badge->Basket ribbon only: **62/72**

Asking one leg to identify the *whole tee+basket pair* is deliberately unfair; the two halves are complementary. Combining front+back features gives 53/72 pair rank-1.

Then applying exact one-to-one ownership independently to the two learned leg potentials gives:

- tee local: 60/72 -> one-to-one: **64/72**
- basket local: 62/72 -> one-to-one: **69/72**
- both endpoints correct together: 61/72
- synthetic Dash: 18/18 after one-to-one
- synthetic Towne Lake: 18/18 after one-to-one

Again: these are synthetic-hard-rival results, not D08/Dev72 assignment results. The architectural implication is the useful part: Tee->Badge and Badge->Basket evidence are strong enough to model separately, then let ownership constraints combine them.

### 7. Things that did not work

- learned average cross-section template alone: 13-19/72
- shallow decision trees: poor
- shallow random forest: 33/72
- ExtraTrees depth 2: 48/72
- empirical histogram Naive Bayes: 44-50/72
- one-step hard-negative mining: 40-42/72 after the larger feature bank
- dumping all longitudinal/tied features into one RBF: no gain; removing some of them improves transfer

The discriminator is therefore not “looks like the average ribbon.” Lower tails, persistence, paired boundaries, relative scale, and leg ownership carry more useful information.

## Strongest hypotheses to take to exact D08 replay

1. **Separate leg potentials.** Fit/score Tee->Badge evidence for tee ownership and Badge->Basket evidence for basket ownership; do not require one scalar pair score to rediscover this decomposition.
2. **Two-rail lower-tail kernel.** Preserve `paired_edge_q25` / low-persistence testimony instead of only aligned support means.
3. **Ribbon persistence kernel.** `contrast_frac_low` is an absurdly strong single feature in the discovery scaffold.
4. **U-shaped evidence transforms.** Prefer a tiny spline / quadratic likelihood ratio over another hand-picked monotonic factor.
5. **Truth-free relative calibration.** Test per-course or per-badge ranks/z-scores for renderer/basemap nuisance removal.
6. **Ownership after testimony.** High-confidence leg associations plus one-to-one/local-region matching deserve a direct exact-D08 ablation.
7. **Do not promote extra complexity.** Five spline knots and hard-negative mining both regressed immediately.
